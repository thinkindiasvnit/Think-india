--------------------------------------------------

dark gray

Typography:
Use a modern geometric/sans-serif font.
Headings should be extremely bold and editorial.
Body text should be clean and highly readable.

Use large typography and generous whitespace.

Border radius:
12–28px for cards and images.
Pill buttons for CTAs.

Animations/prototype:
- subtle fade-up on section entrance
- image scale slightly on hover
- buttons should have subtle hover movement
- cards should gently lift on hover
- navigation remains minimal

Responsive:
Create desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.

The final result should look like a high-end youth organization / innovation initiative website, not a conventional college website.
